The problem

A new city shouldn't feel
like a gamble.

Every year millions of Indians travel somewhere new and end up guessing: who to trust, what's worth seeing, what a fair price is.

Strangers at the station

Touts, unverified drivers and "special rates" are often the first welcome a traveler gets.

Tourist-trap plans

Generic packages skip the places locals actually love and rush you through the rest.

Nobody to call

When something goes wrong far from home, there's rarely a trusted person on your side.

The idea

What if every traveler had a trusted local friend in every city, someone verified, rated and one tap away?

That's GoLoc. Real locals earn from what they know best, and travelers get a city's best side, safely.

Captains

Take care of everything: pickups, stays, transport and full trip packages, from simple to lavish.

Guides
