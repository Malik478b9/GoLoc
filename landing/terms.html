5. How booking works

  1. Request. You choose a Captain's service, your dates, the number of guests and, for packages, a comfort tier (Normal, Medium or Lavish), then send a booking request. You're not charged at this point.
  2. Captain responds. The Captain has 24 hours to accept or decline. If they don't respond, the request expires.
  3. Pay. Once the request is accepted, you have 2 hours to pay in the app. If you don't pay in time, the booking expires and the dates are released.
  4. Confirmed. After payment the booking is confirmed. You get a start PIN to give the Captain when you meet, which officially starts the trip.

The price shown before you pay is the full amount: the Captain's fare for your group and dates, plus the trip cover fee where one is shown. Prices include applicable taxes unless we say otherwise.

Itineraries, including those drafted with GoLoc's AI tools, are plans prepared by the Captain. Timings, entry tickets, prices and hotel availability can change, so please check important details with your Captain before you travel.

6. Payments

  • Payments are processed by our payment partner Razorpay. GoLoc never sees or stores your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN.
  • GoLoc collects the payment on behalf of the Captain. The Captain's share, which is the fare minus GoLoc's commission, is released to them only after the trip is completed.
  • Pay only inside the app. Never pay a Captain in cash or directly for a GoLoc booking. Off-platform payments aren't covered by our refunds, trip cover, live tracking or SOS. Please report any Captain who asks you to pay outside the app.

8. Safety features

  • Live trip tracking, the SOS button and trip sharing are available on paid, in-app bookings while the trip is in progress.
  • SOS doesn't replace emergency services. In an emergency, always call 112 (all emergencies), 100 (police) or 108 (ambulance) first. GoLoc's SOS alerts our team and shows nearby emergency services, but we can't guarantee how quickly anyone responds.
  • Location features depend on your phone, its settings and network coverage, which are outside our control.
  • Where a trip cover fee is charged, the cover is provided by the licensed insurance partner named on your booking, under that insurer's own terms. GoLoc is not an insurer.
